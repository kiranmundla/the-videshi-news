About

Saffron Kitchen & Bar has been a cornerstone of the Bay Area's Indian dining scene since 2008. Specializing in authentic South Indian cuisine with a modern twist, the restaurant sources spices directly from Kerala and Tamil Nadu. Their weekend brunch buffet has become a beloved gathering point for the local Indian community, featuring over 40 dishes rotating weekly. The restaurant also offers full-service catering for weddings, pujas, and corporate events, with packages for 50–500 guests.
