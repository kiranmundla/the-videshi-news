## Why Switzerland?

Switzerland is the Bollywood dream destination that actually lives up to the hype. Generations of Indians fell in love with its Alpine meadows through Yash Chopra films, and the reality is, if anything, more spectacular than the screen. For the Indian diaspora, Switzerland represents aspirational Europe — clean, efficient, safe, and staggeringly beautiful. It is also one of the few European countries that has actively courted Indian tourists, with Hindi signage at Jungfraujoch and vegetarian options at mountain restaurants. Whether you are recreating a DDLJ scene or skiing for the first time, Switzerland delivers a precision-engineered holiday.

## Top Experiences

**1. Jungfraujoch, Top of Europe** — A cogwheel railway climbs to 3,454 metres through the Eiger's interior. The observation deck offers views across the Aletsch Glacier. Yes, the "Bollywood platform" with Hindi signs is real. Go early on a clear day — check the webcam before booking the CHF 200+ ticket.

**2. Lake Lucerne Boat Cruise** — A paddle steamer across Switzerland's most beautiful lake, ringed by mountains. Combine it with the Mount Pilatus golden round trip — boat, cogwheel train, cable car, and panorama gondola in a loop. An engineering marvel and a visual feast.

**3. Interlaken Adventure Sports** — The adventure capital of Europe. Paragliding over the lake with Alpine peaks behind you is a life-changing 15 minutes. Also available: canyoning, skydiving, bungee jumping, and river rafting. Book through Outdoor Interlaken for reputable operators.

**4. Zermatt & the Matterhorn** — The iconic pyramid-shaped peak is Switzerland's calling card. Zermatt is car-free and postcard-perfect. Take the Gornergrat Railway to the viewing platform for the classic Matterhorn panorama reflected in Riffelsee lake. Skiing here in winter is world-class.

**5. Grindelwald First Cliff Walk** — A metal walkway bolted into a cliff face 2,000 metres above the valley floor. The First Flyer zip line (800 metres long) and the First Glider (eagle-eye chairlift) add adrenaline. The views of Eiger's north face are absurd. Family-friendly and accessible by gondola.

## Getting There

Zürich is the main international gateway, with connections from Delhi (8 hours via direct Air India seasonal service) or via Frankfurt, Munich, or Dubai. Geneva serves western Switzerland. From the US, Swiss Air flies direct from New York, Chicago, and San Francisco. Switzerland's rail system is Europe's best — buy a Swiss Travel Pass for unlimited trains, buses, boats, and many mountain railways. The pass pays for itself in two days of travel.

## Where to Stay

Swiss hotels are expensive but immaculate. Budget CHF 200–400 per night for mid-range in tourist areas. Interlaken, Lucerne, and Zermatt have the most options. Youth hostels (Swiss Youth Hostels association) are clean and well-run at CHF 40–80. Airbnb chalets in Grindelwald or Lauterbrunnen offer stunning valley-floor locations. For luxury, the Bürgenstock Resort above Lake Lucerne and the Grand Hotel Zermatterhof set the standard. Many Indian tour operators package Switzerland with Paris — consider separating them to allow deeper exploration. Three to four nights is the minimum to do justice.

## NRI Tips

Indian passport holders need a Schengen visa — apply through VFS Global at least four weeks in advance. US citizens enter visa-free for 90 days. The Swiss Franc (CHF) is strong — roughly 1 CHF per US dollar. Credit cards work everywhere except some mountain huts. Tipping is included in bills but rounding up is appreciated. Switzerland is multilingual — German in the north and east, French in the west, Italian in the south. English is widely spoken in tourist areas. Vegetarian food is available but limited outside Indian restaurants — cheese, bread, and rösti will feature heavily. Summer (June–September) is hiking season; winter (December–March) is skiing season. Spring and autumn are shoulder seasons with lower prices and fewer crowds. Pack layers — mountain weather changes within minutes.
