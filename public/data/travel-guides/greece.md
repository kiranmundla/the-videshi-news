## Why Greece?

Greece is where honeymooning NRI couples go when they want photos that make Instagram weep. The white-washed buildings against impossibly blue water in Santorini have launched a thousand engagement announcements. But Greece is far more than its most famous postcard — it is ancient ruins that predate everything in your history textbook, islands with personalities as distinct as Indian states, and a food culture built on olive oil, grilled meats, and vegetables that tastes nothing like what passes for "Greek food" in American restaurants. For Indian Americans, Greece offers Europe's warmth (both thermal and emotional) at prices significantly below Paris or London.

## Top Experiences

**1. Santorini Sunset at Oia** — Position yourself at the castle ruins in Oia by 6 PM. The sunset here is objectively one of the best in the world. Stay in a cave hotel in Fira or Oia for the full caldera view. Swim at Red Beach and Perissa's black sand.

**2. Athens: Acropolis & Parthenon** — The Acropolis is to Greece what the Taj Mahal is to India — the one thing you cannot skip. Visit at opening (8 AM) or late afternoon to avoid midday heat. The new Acropolis Museum at the base is world-class.

**3. Mykonos: Beaches & Little Venice** — The party island of Greece, but also genuinely beautiful. Little Venice's waterfront bars at sunset, the iconic windmills, and Paradise Beach are the highlights. Excellent for couples and friend groups.

**4. Meteora Monasteries** — Six Byzantine monasteries perched on towering rock pillars in central Greece. Less visited than the islands but equally jaw-dropping. A day trip from Kalabaka — hire a driver or join a sunrise tour.

**5. Crete: Samaria Gorge & Elafonisi Beach** — Greece's largest island has its own mountain range, its own cuisine, and Europe's longest gorge hike (16 km). Elafonisi Beach — with its pink sand and shallow turquoise lagoon — feels like it belongs in the Caribbean.

## Getting There

Athens has direct flights from New York (JFK) on Delta and Emirates, and from Philadelphia on American Airlines. Flight time is 10–11 hours. From Athens, ferries and short flights connect to the islands — Athens to Santorini by ferry takes 5–8 hours (high-speed) or 35 minutes by plane. Book inter-island ferries on Ferryhopper.com. The island-hopping route Athens → Mykonos → Santorini → Crete is the classic NRI itinerary. Budget two days minimum per island.

## Where to Stay

Santorini: Oia for sunsets and luxury (Katikies, Mystique), Fira for central access and better prices, Imerovigli for quiet with caldera views. Mykonos: Mykonos Town for nightlife, Ornos for families. Athens: Plaka neighbourhood — walking distance to everything. Crete: Chania old town is the most charming base. Greek hotels range from €50 boutique cave-rooms to €500+ luxury suites. Shoulder season (May, September–October) offers the best value — 30–40% cheaper than July–August.

## Food & the Desi Connection

Greek food shares surprising parallels with Indian cuisine — both centre on yoghurt, grilled meats, flatbreads, and vegetables. Tzatziki is essentially raita with garlic. Souvlaki is seekh kebab's Mediterranean cousin. Must-try: moussaka, fresh grilled octopus, spanakopita (spinach pie), and loukoumades (honey-drizzled dough balls — basically gulab jamun's Greek cousin). Vegetarians thrive in Greece — horiatiki salad, dolmades, grilled halloumi, and fava purée are staples. Breakfast is light — coffee and a pastry — but lunch and dinner are generous multi-course affairs.

## NRI Tips

Indian passport holders need a Schengen visa — the same visa that covers Italy, France, and 24 other countries. Apply through VFS Global at the Greek consulate. US citizens get 90 days visa-free. Currency is the Euro. Cards are accepted at most restaurants and hotels; cash needed for small tavernas and ferries. Greece is extremely safe — crime rates among the lowest in Europe. The sun is intense — SPF 50, hats, and hydration are non-negotiable from May to September. Islands get very windy (the Meltemi wind blows July–August) — ferries may be cancelled. Book ferries and accommodation early for July and August. The best months are May to June and September to October — warm water, fewer crowds, and lower prices.
