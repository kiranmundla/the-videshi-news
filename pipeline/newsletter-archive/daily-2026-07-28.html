Keigo Higashino, Japanese Mystery Master Beloved Across Asia, Dies at 68
  • Keigo Higashino, Japan's bestselling mystery novelist and the most popular novelist in China, died of colorectal cancer on July 23 at age 68
  • His novel The Devotion of Suspect X was adapted into the 2023 Netflix India film Jaane Jaan, starring Kareena Kapoor Khan, which became the most-viewed Indian film on the platform globally
  • Higashino won every major Japanese literary award including the Naoki Prize, and nearly 20 of his works were adapted into films and TV series
  • He served as president of the Mystery Writers of Japan from 2009 to 2013 and published over 80 novels across a four-decade career

Keigo Higashino, the Japanese mystery novelist whose intricately plotted crime fiction earned him a readership stretching from Tokyo to Mumbai, died on July 23 from colorectal cancer. He was 68.

His funeral was held privately by his family, according to Japanese media reports.
