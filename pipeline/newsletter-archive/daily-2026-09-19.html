Grated Veggies, Blended Tofu: A 23-Gram Protein Savoury Pancake Recipe
  • Fitness influencer Vanshika Khurana (Fit.Khurana) shared a savoury pancake she says delivers 23 grams of protein, built on 160 g of blended silken tofu and 35 g of rice flour.
  • A full cup of shredded zucchini, carrot, onion and cabbage goes into the batter, — fibre without a plate of raw vegetables.
  • Blend the tofu smooth, fold it into a seasoned rice-flour batter with the grated vegetables, cook thin and crisp in a lightly oiled pan, and serve with a soy-lemon dressing.
  • Hindustan Times reports the recipe from an Instagram video shared on September 8, and notes it has not independently verified the nutrition claims.

A savoury pancake built around 23 grams of protein

Fitness influencer Vanshika Khurana, who posts as Fit.Khurana, shared this savoury pancake — shredded vegetables folded into a tofu-and-rice-flour batter, cooked crisp and served with a soy-lemon dipping sauce — in an Instagram video on September 8. According to Hindustan Times, Khurana says she lost 40 kilos and reversed PCOS without giving up the food she enjoys.

Her pitch targets reluctant vegetable eaters: grate whatever sits in the fridge and bury it in batter.
