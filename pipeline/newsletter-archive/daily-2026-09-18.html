SEMICON India 2026: New Delhi Pushes to Localise Chip Materials and Gases
  • At SEMICON India 2026 in New Delhi, the government's focus has shifted from winning fabrication projects to building the domestic supply chain beneath them — local suppliers of materials, gases, chemicals, equipment sub-systems and design IP.
  • The Centre is running targeted matchmaking roundtables between Indian manufacturers and suppliers from the US, Japan, Singapore, South Korea, Malaysia and Europe during the Sept. 17-19 summit.
  • Merck, Applied Materials, Lam Research and ASML all made India supply-chain announcements on day one, including Applied Materials' $5 billion "India Vision 2035" and Lam's roughly Rs 100 billion silicon-component plant.
  • Semicon 2.0, approved in July with a ₹1.275 lakh crore outlay, formalises the shift: six pillars covering design, equipment and materials, fabs, advanced packaging, R&D and talent.

India's semiconductor strategy is moving down the stack.
