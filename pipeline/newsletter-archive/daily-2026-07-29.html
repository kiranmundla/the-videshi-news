Toyota Launches 9th-Generation Hilux in India Starting at Rs 31.99 Lakh
  • Toyota has launched the 9th-generation Hilux in India at Rs 31.99–36.69 lakh (ex-showroom), with test drives starting July 29
  • A 4x2 drivetrain option is available for the first time, lowering the entry point for buyers who don't need off-road capability
  • The cabin receives dual 12.3-inch screens, a JBL audio system, and advanced safety tech including Blind Spot Monitor and Pre-collision Safety
  • The 2.8-litre turbo-diesel engine returns with 204 PS and 500 Nm, paired exclusively with a 6-speed automatic transmission

A Ground-Up Redesign for a Global Workhorse

Toyota Kirloskar Motor (TKM) has taken the wraps off the 9th-generation Hilux in India, pricing the lifestyle pickup from Rs 31.99 lakh for the new GX 4x2 AT variant to Rs 36.69 lakh for the range-topping VX 4x4 AT, both ex-showroom. The launch, which took place on July 28, arrives roughly eight months after the truck's global debut, according to MotorBeam.
