FDA Approves First OTC Pill Combining Tylenol and Naproxen for 12-Hour Pain Relief
  • The FDA on July 24 approved Kenvue's Tylenol with Naproxen, the first OTC pill combining acetaminophen and naproxen sodium in a single dose.
  • Each dose contains 650 mg of acetaminophen and 220 mg of naproxen sodium, providing up to 12 hours of non-opioid pain relief.
  • Clinical studies across eight trials showed the combination delivered superior pain relief compared to either drug taken alone.
  • The FDA granted Kenvue three years of market exclusivity for the new formulation, which will soon be available at major U.S. retailers.
