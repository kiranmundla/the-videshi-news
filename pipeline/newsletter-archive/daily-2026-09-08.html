📈 MARKETS — TOP STORY
India's 7.8% GDP Growth Faces Scrutiny Over Methods and Meaning
  • India's April-June GDP growth of 7.8% beat forecasts but triggered a debate over revised base-year data and the accuracy of price deflators
  • Former finance secretary Subhash Chandra Garg argued the figure was inflated by downward revisions to year-ago data, while ex-RBI governor Raghuram Rajan questioned why strong GDP has not translated into more jobs and investment
  • The statistics ministry defended its methodology, citing a new Producer Price Index with over 300 deflators and additional data sources
  • Business strategist Vivek Singhal argues the deeper issue is that GDP alone cannot measure human capability and sustainable prosperity

The Numbers That Sparked the Fight

India's economy grew 7.8% in the April-June quarter, comfortably beating the 7.1% forecast by analysts in a Reuters poll. Investment and manufacturing strength added to solid consumer demand, making the world's fifth-largest economy one of the fastest-growing major markets.
