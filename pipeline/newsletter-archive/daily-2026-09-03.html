Yash's 'Toxic' Closes Week One at Rs 314 Crore Worldwide Amid Profitability Concerns
  • Yash's "Toxic: A Fairy Tale for Grown Ups" closed its first week with Rs 229.95 crore net in India and Rs 313.55 crore worldwide, powered by a massive Rs 101.10 crore opening day.
  • Hindi collections account for roughly Rs 123-125 crore of the domestic total, but daily drops have been steep — from Rs 50 crore on Day 1 to Rs 4 crore by the first Monday.
  • With an estimated production budget of Rs 300-400 crore, the film needs to cross Rs 500 crore worldwide to be profitable, according to trade analysts.
  • Week two faces additional pressure from the September 4 theatrical release of "Mirzapur: The Movie," which targets the same Hindi-belt audience.

Yash's Toxic: A Fairy Tale for Grown Ups wrapped its first week of release with a worldwide gross of Rs 313.55 crore, according to Sacnilk data. The Kannada-language film, directed by Geetu Mohandas, collected Rs 229.95 crore net domestically across all languages, with an additional Rs 40.75 crore from overseas markets.
