CDC Declares Record Lettuce-Linked Parasite Outbreak Over After 12,883 Cases
  • The CDC declared the multistate cyclosporiasis outbreak linked to iceberg lettuce over on Friday, Sept. 11, after 12,883 confirmed cases across 21 states.
  • The outbreak is the largest on record in the U.S., causing at least 570 hospitalizations and two deaths, both reported in Michigan.
  • Traceback pointed to shredded iceberg lettuce from Taylor Farms de Mexico; the company recalled all iceberg lettuce sourced from central Mexico on July 18.
  • The FDA's investigation continues, with laboratory analysis of samples from Mexican growers pending and at least five other outbreak clusters still open.

Largest recorded outbreak ends after four months

The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention declared an end Friday to the multistate outbreak of cyclosporiasis tied to iceberg lettuce, closing the book on the largest recorded surge of the parasitic illness in U.S.
