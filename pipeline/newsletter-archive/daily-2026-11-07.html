Top Story
India Has Cancer Screening. Fewer Than 2 in 100 Women Have Ever Used It
Why it matters
The dismal uptake of cancer screening among Indian women highlights a critical gap in health awareness and access, leading to preventable deaths and suffering. For the Indian diaspora in the US, this underscores the urgent need for advocacy and support for health education initiatives that can empower communities to prioritize early detection, ultimately improving survival rates and quality of life. Addressing these disparities is not just a public health issue; it's a matter of social responsibility that can influence policy and investment in healthcare infrastructure back home.
