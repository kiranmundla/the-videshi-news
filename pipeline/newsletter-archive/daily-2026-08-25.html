Bengaluru's Airbound Raises $37M to Build Autonomous Drones That Could Rival Trucking Costs
  • Bengaluru-based Airbound has raised $37 million in Series A funding led by Greenoaks, with DoorDash, Lightspeed, and Lachy Groom participating, bringing total funding to approximately $50 million.
  • The startup has completed over 13,000 autonomous flights, including 1,000+ with Narayana Health transporting diagnostic samples between facilities in minutes instead of hours.
  • Airbound has signed an agreement with the Andhra Pradesh government for a three-city drone delivery network targeting 10,000 flights per day across retail, e-commerce, and healthcare.
  • The company aims to build drones capable of carrying up to 100 kg of cargo at costs competitive with fully loaded trucks — a goal investors say could resolve the tension between fast and cheap delivery.

Airbound, a Bengaluru-based autonomous drone startup founded in 2023, has raised $37 million in a Series A round led by Greenoaks, as it pushes to make drone delivery as cheap as trucking across India's sprawling logistics network.

The round drew participation from DoorDash, Lightspeed, Humba Ventures, and Lachy Groom — founder of robotics company Physical Intelligence — bringing Airbound's total funding to approximately $50 million. The company had previously raised $8.65 million in a seed round in October 2025, also led by Groom.

A Rocket-Like Design for Last-Mile Delivery

Airbound's drones use a vertical-flight, tail-sitter design — launching and landing like a rocket before transitioning to efficient horizontal flight.
