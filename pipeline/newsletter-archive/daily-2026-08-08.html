🛂 IMMIGRATION — TOP STORY
350,000 Haitians Face Deportation as Court Lifts Final TPS Protection
  • A federal judge officially ended TPS protections for roughly 350,000 Haitians, clearing the way for deportation to a country the State Department has repeatedly warned is dangerous and unstable.
  • ICE has already begun targeting former TPS holders, fitting some with ankle monitors, as DHS pledges aggressive enforcement.
  • The Supreme Court's 6-3 ruling in June stripped courts of authority to review TPS termination decisions, potentially affecting 1.3 million immigrants from 17 countries.
  • Nearly 190,000 Haitian TPS holders were employed in the U.S., contributing an estimated $5.9 billion to the economy, with the elder care industry facing the most immediate disruption.

Court Ends Months of Legal Uncertainty

A federal judge on August 5 officially confirmed that temporary deportation protections for hundreds of thousands of Haitians are no longer in effect, marking the final legal step in a months-long battle that reached the Supreme Court and now clears the path for one of the largest immigration enforcement campaigns in recent U.S. District Judge Ana Reyes lifted the stay that had paused the Department of Homeland Security's termination of Haiti's Temporary Protected Status designation.
