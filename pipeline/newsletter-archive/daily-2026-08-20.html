🛂 IMMIGRATION — TOP STORY
DHS Proposes Eliminating 60-Day Grace Period for H-1B Workers After Job Loss
  • DHS has submitted a proposed rule to the White House that would eliminate the 60-day grace period for H-1B, L-1, O-1, TN, and other employment-based visa holders after job loss.
  • The rule is under OMB review and has not been published yet — the grace period remains in effect during the rulemaking process.
  • If finalized, workers who lose their jobs would have to leave the U.S. immediately or face being out of status, with no window to find a new employer or change visa category.

What the Proposed Rule Would Do

The Department of Homeland Security has advanced a proposed regulation (RIN: 1615-AD22) to the White House Office of Information and Regulatory Affairs (OIRA) that would eliminate the 60-day grace period currently available to certain nonimmigrant workers following the end of their employment.
