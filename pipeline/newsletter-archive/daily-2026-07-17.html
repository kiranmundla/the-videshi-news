US Sanctions Bill Could Hit India With 100% Tariff Over Russian Oil Purchases
Key Takeaways
  • The Sanctioning Russia Act would authorise 100% tariffs on imports from the top 5 buyers of Russian crude, including India.
  • The bill has 26 Senate co-sponsors and Trump's backing, giving it a strong path to passage.
  • India's Russian crude imports already fell from 1.84 million to 1.04 million barrels/day earlier this year.
  • Economists estimate a worst-case scenario could shave up to 0.5% off India's GDP.
  • A US Treasury waiver that shielded Indian purchases expired on June 17, 2026.
A bipartisan group of US senators on Tuesday introduced an updated version of the Russia sanctions bill that could subject India to tariffs of up to 100% on goods entering the United States — all because of New Delhi's continued purchases of Russian crude oil, Reuters reported. The legislation, known as the Sanctioning Russia Act, was championed by the late Senator Lindsey Graham, who died suddenly over the weekend, and co-authored by Connecticut Democrat Richard Blumenthal.
