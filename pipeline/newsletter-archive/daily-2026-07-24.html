🛂 IMMIGRATION — TOP STORY
Canada PGWP Refusal Rate Hits 25% as Portal Flaws Trip Up Indian Graduates
  • Canada's PGWP refusal rate climbed past 25% by late 2025, up sharply from prior years, with most rejections linked to missing language test documents.
  • IRCC's application portal lacks a dedicated upload field for mandatory language results introduced in November 2024, causing graduates to omit documents they actually possessed.
  • Affected graduates have three options — reconsideration via IRCC webform, judicial review within 15 days, or a new application — but strict deadlines make immediate legal advice critical.
  • Indian students face compounded risk as Canada's TRIS fraud-screening framework routes applications from high-risk countries through heightened scrutiny, including PGWP applications from former SDS permit holders.

Canada's Post-Graduation Work Permit refusal rate has climbed past 25%, according to immigration analysts and internal projections, turning what was once a routine pathway to Canadian work experience into a minefield for international graduates — a disproportionate number of them Indian.

The surge in rejections is not driven by ineligible applicants. Immigration lawyers across Canada say most refusals stem from a single systemic failure: the Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) online application portal does not include a dedicated upload field for language test results, a document that became mandatory for all PGWP applicants on November 1, 2024.

"Refusals are being issued through an automated system, resulting in applicants being refused en masse for failing to upload a document they were never prompted to upload during the application process."

— Will Tao, Vancouver immigration lawyer, quoted in the Toronto Star

A Portal Problem, Not an Eligibility Problem

Under rules introduced in late 2024, all PGWP applicants must submit proof of English or French language proficiency — CLB 7 for university degree holders, CLB 5 for college diploma and certificate graduates.
