Sonal Dinusha's 1,139-Minute Marathon Denies India Series Sweep in Sri Lanka
  • Sri Lanka's Sonal Dinusha scored 420 runs across four innings, spending 1,139 minutes at the crease to deny India a 2-0 series sweep.
  • Dinusha made 133 not out in the fourth innings — his third hundred in four innings — to help Sri Lanka draw the second Test at the Sinhalese Sports Club Ground in Colombo.
  • He became only the third batter in Test history to score twin centuries in a match where his team was asked to follow on.
  • India won the series 1-0 but captain Shubman Gill acknowledged his side was outplayed on the final day, with the team still needing to win six of their remaining seven Tests to control their World Test Championship destiny.

A Rearguard for the Ages

Sonal Dinusha produced one of the most defiant batting performances in recent Test cricket history, spending 1,139 minutes at the crease across the two-Test series to single-handedly deny India a clean sweep in Sri Lanka.

The 25-year-old scored 420 runs across four innings — 722 balls, three centuries — and his unbeaten 133 in the final innings at Colombo's Sinhalese Sports Club Ground on August 27 ensured Sri Lanka salvaged a draw after being asked to follow on. India won the series 1-0 after taking the first Test in Galle.

Dinusha forged a critical century partnership with Keshara Nuwantha to frustrate India through the morning session on the final day.
