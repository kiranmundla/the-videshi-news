Kanpur BTech Gold Medallist Dies by Suicide After 50 Failed Government Job Attempts
  • A BTech gold medallist from Kanpur died by suicide after failing to secure a government job despite appearing for more than 50 competitive examinations.
  • The young man left a suicide note addressed to his father saying 'Sorry papa, I tried,' drawing nationwide attention to India's unemployment crisis among educated youth.
  • India's government job vacancy pipeline remains severely choked, with millions of candidates competing for a fraction of available positions each year.
  • The tragedy adds to a growing list of suicides linked to examination pressure and unemployment, prompting renewed calls for mental health support and recruitment reform.

A BTech gold medallist from Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, has died by suicide after failing to secure a government job despite sitting for more than 50 competitive examinations, police said on Friday. The young man left behind a handwritten note addressed to his father that read, "Sorry papa, I tried" — words that have since ricocheted across Indian social media and reignited a fierce debate over the country's broken public-sector hiring system.

A Promising Graduate, a Relentless Search

The deceased, an engineering graduate who had earned a gold medal for academic excellence during his BTech programme, had spent years preparing for government recruitment examinations after completing his degree.
