Kerala Reports 105 Chikungunya Cases as Monsoon Fuels Outbreak Fears
  • Kerala has reported 105 confirmed chikungunya cases across four districts: Thiruvananthapuram, Alappuzha, Ernakulam, and Kozhikode
  • The outbreak coincides with peak monsoon season, which creates ideal mosquito breeding conditions
  • Health authorities have deployed entomologists, epidemiologists, and medical specialists; each affected district received ₹5 lakh for prevention efforts
  • Two FDA-approved chikungunya vaccines exist for adults — travelers to Kerala should consult their doctors before visiting

Kerala's Health Department has confirmed 105 cases of chikungunya fever across four districts, prompting intensified prevention measures as the state's monsoon season creates optimal conditions for the Aedes mosquitoes that transmit the virus.

Four Districts Affected

The confirmed cases span Thiruvananthapuram, Alappuzha, Ernakulam, and Kozhikode — districts that together cover much of Kerala's coastline and are popular destinations for NRI visitors during the monsoon and Onam festival season. NDTV reported the case count at 105, while News18 highlighted concerns about symptoms in children that parents should watch for.

Kerala Health Minister Sreemathy addressed allegations of a delayed response, clarifying that deaths reported in the Cherthala taluk of Alappuzha district were caused by leptospirosis and underlying conditions such as heart disease, not chikungunya.
