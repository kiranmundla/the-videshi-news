📈 MARKETS — TOP STORY
Paytm Shares Slide as NPCI Confirms 0.4% UPI Fee Above Rs 2,000
  • The National Payments Corporation of India will levy a 0.4% fee on UPI payments to merchants above Rs 2,000 starting October 15 — the first merchant charge ever imposed on the network, Reuters reported.
  • A government gazette notification issued Monday bars banks and payment system providers from imposing any charge, direct or indirect, on UPI transactions or RuPay debit card payments of up to Rs 2,000.
  • Shares of One 97 Communications, Paytm's parent, fell more than 2.5% to Rs 1,761.80 on the NSE on Tuesday, one session after the stock hit a fresh 52-week high of Rs 1,840.
  • Brokerages are bullish on the shift: Jefferies holds a Buy with a Rs 2,100 target and Bernstein names Paytm its top pick with a Rs 2,200 target, citing MDR as an earnings driver.

A line in the sand at Rs 2,000

India's experiment with entirely free digital payments is about to end — at least for bigger purchases. The National Payments Corporation of India said on Tuesday it would introduce a 0.4% fee on payments to merchants above Rs 2,000 on its UPI network, effective October 15, according to Reuters.
