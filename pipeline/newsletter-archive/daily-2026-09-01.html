A Strategic Bet on the Back End

As geopolitical tensions fracture global semiconductor supply chains, India is making a deliberate strategic choice: rather than chasing the capital-prohibitive race for leading-edge logic chips, the country is building itself into a critical hub for back-end operations and specialized analog production.

According to PwC analysis, regions are now specializing rather than competing across all semiconductor segments. India's focus on OSAT (outsourced semiconductor assembly and test), analog chips, and microcontrollers positions it in sectors essential for the automotive and industrial markets — segments that constitute the overwhelming majority of the 1.05 trillion chips sold globally each year, according to DQ India.
