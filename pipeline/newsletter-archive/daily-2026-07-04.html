America Rejected 61 Per Cent of Indian Student Visas Last Year. The Pipeline Is Bleeding Out
Key Takeaways
  • 61% of Indian F-1 visa applications were rejected in 2025, the highest rate in a decade.
  • Indian students face a denial rate nearly seven times higher than European applicants.
  • Only 22,000 F-1 visas were issued to Indian students in 2025, a 60% drop from the previous year.
  • International students contributed $44 billion to the US economy in 2023-24, highlighting economic impacts.
  • Proposed policy changes could further restrict F-1 visa holders' duration and options for work authorization.

Shorelight analysis of US State Department data
For every hundred Indian students who applied for an F-1 visa in 2025, sixty-one were turned away. The rejection rate — confirmed by a Shorelight analysis of a decade of US State Department data — is the highest India has recorded in ten years, up from 53 per cent in 2024, 36 per cent in 2023, and roughly 23 per cent in 2015.
