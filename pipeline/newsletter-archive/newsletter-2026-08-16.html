✍️ EDITOR'S DESK — TOP STORY
India at 80: Celebrating Freedom, Confronting the Future
  • PM Modi delivered his 12th consecutive Independence Day address — 103 minutes, the longest ever from the Red Fort — announcing Made-in-India semiconductors, Mission Sudarshan Chakra, a $10 trillion economy roadmap, and declaring the Indus Waters Treaty unacceptable.
  • India's economy grew 7.6% in FY2026, the fastest among G20 nations for the fourth consecutive year, with GDP crossing $4 trillion, UPI processing 14 billion monthly transactions, and ISRO preparing for its first crewed space mission.
  • Dubai's Burj Khalifa lit up in the Indian tricolour, while diaspora celebrations from San Jose to San Diego marked the day — Indian Americans are now the highest-earning ethnic group in America with $100B+ in annual remittances.
  • Significant challenges persist: per capita income ranks 139th globally, youth unemployment exceeds 20%, press freedom has fallen to 159th of 180 countries, and air pollution claims over a million lives annually.
As the tricolour unfurls over the ramparts of the Red Fort on this August 15, India marks a milestone that would have seemed improbable to the freedom fighters who stood at those same walls 80 years ago. The nation they imagined — sovereign, democratic, and self-reliant — has become the world's most populous country, the fifth-largest economy, and a rising power whose voice carries weight from the UN Security Council to the G20.
