Who Is Shailesh Jejurikar? The Mumbai Boy Who Shared a Classroom With Satya Nadella — and Now Runs a $380 Billion American Giant

When Procter & Gamble named Shailesh Jejurikar its president and CEO effective January 1, 2026, the announcement did more than shuffle a corporate org chart. It placed an Indian-born, IIM-educated executive at the helm of one of America’s oldest and most iconic consumer goods empires — a company that has been in continuous operation for 187 years and touches the daily routines of roughly five billion people worldwide.

Jejurikar, 58, is the first Asian-born CEO in P&G’s history, and only the second non-American to hold the title.
