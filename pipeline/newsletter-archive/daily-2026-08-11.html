India's Russian Oil Imports Hit Record 2.8 Million Barrels Per Day in July
  • India imported a record 2.8 million barrels per day of Russian crude in July 2026, making up 55.5% of the country's total crude imports
  • Russian crude shipments to India were worth €5.5 billion in July — a 2.1% volume increase over June's previous record — with smaller ports driving the surge
  • Indian refineries continue exporting refined petroleum products worth hundreds of millions of euros to countries that have sanctioned Moscow, including the EU and the United States

A New Monthly Record

India's crude oil purchases from Russia reached an all-time high of 2.8 million barrels per day (bpd) in July 2026, according to data compiled by the Centre for Research on Energy and Clean Air (CREA). The figure represents roughly 55.5% of India's total crude imports of just over 5 million bpd — and marks the second consecutive month in which Russian crude imports set a record.

In value terms, India imported Russian crude worth €5.5 billion during July, with crude oil accounting for 87% of the country's total Russian fossil fuel purchases of €6.4 billion.
