Honda India Two-Wheeler Sales Hit 5.43 Lakh Units in July, Eyes Premium Push
  • Honda Motorcycle & Scooter India sold 5,42,934 two-wheelers in July 2026, a 5% year-on-year increase from 5,15,378 units in July 2025.
  • Domestic sales grew a modest 2% to 4,76,436 units, while exports surged 35% to 66,498 units on the back of strong demand from overseas markets.
  • Honda has unveiled 10 new products for India — including the ADV 160 adventure scooter, Rebel 300 cruiser and locally-manufactured 500cc motorcycles — marking its most aggressive premium expansion in years.

Honda Motorcycle & Scooter India (HMSI) reported total wholesale dispatches of 5,42,934 units in July 2026, a 5% year-on-year increase over 5,15,378 units shipped during the same month last year, according to figures released by the company. The numbers represent a sequential improvement from June 2026, when Honda dispatched 5,28,281 units.

Domestic sales stood at 4,76,436 units, up a modest 2% from 4,66,331 units in July 2025.
