Bangladesh Measles Death Toll Crosses 1,050 in Deadliest Outbreak in Decades
  • Bangladesh's measles death toll has reached 1,053, with five more children dying in the previous 24 hours, according to the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS).
  • Since March 15, the country has recorded 175,198 suspected cases and 20,282 lab-confirmed infections, with 100 confirmed deaths and hundreds more suspected — the vast majority among unvaccinated children under two.
  • It is the deadliest measles outbreak Bangladesh has seen since 2005, driven by Covid-era immunization gaps, delayed catch-up campaigns, and a target population that authorities later admitted was underestimated.
  • An emergency campaign claims 1.97 crore children vaccinated, but cases resurged in August after a June-July dip. Health authorities are preparing a new drive targeting 16 lakh more children with 25 lakh UNICEF vaccines in the pipeline.

The toll keeps rising

Bangladesh's measles crisis deepened this week as the death toll reached 1,053, with five more children dying of measles or measles-like symptoms in a single 24-hour period, the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) reported.
