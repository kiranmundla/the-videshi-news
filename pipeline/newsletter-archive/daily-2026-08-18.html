Anirudh Ravichander and Kavya Maran Wedding Reportedly Set for November 2026
  • Veteran actor Y Gee Mahendra, Anirudh Ravichander's uncle, has publicly confirmed that the music composer and Sunrisers Hyderabad CEO Kavya Maran are set to marry.
  • Multiple reports indicate the wedding is planned for the second week of November 2026, shortly after the October 15 release of Rajinikanth's Jailer 2.
  • The ceremony is reportedly being planned as a destination wedding in Spain, followed by a large-scale reception in Chennai.
  • Neither Anirudh nor Kavya has officially commented on the wedding plans, though the couple has reportedly been together since 2024.

What was once South India's most persistent rumour now appears to be heading toward reality.
