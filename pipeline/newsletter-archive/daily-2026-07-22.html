Air India Launches Non-Stop Delhi-Toronto Flights With New Boeing 787-9
  • Air India will operate non-stop Delhi-Toronto flights from August 1 using its new Boeing 787-9 Dreamliner, eliminating the Vienna fuel stop and cutting travel time from 20 hours to about 17 hours.
  • Daily service will be restored on the route, up from five weekly flights after temporary reductions due to fuel price increases and geopolitical disruptions.
  • The new aircraft features Air India's latest three-class cabin with 30 Business Class suites, 28 Premium Economy seats, and 238 Economy seats — the first time Premium Economy will be available on the Delhi-Toronto route.
  • Canada is home to nearly 1.9 million people of Indian origin, with over 800,000 concentrated in the Greater Toronto Area alone.

Air India announced on Wednesday that it will begin operating non-stop flights between Delhi and Toronto from August 1, deploying its factory-built Boeing 787-9 Dreamliner on the route. The switch eliminates the airline's enroute fuel stop at Vienna, shaving roughly three hours off the westbound journey.

Under the new schedule, flight AI187 will depart Delhi at 1:45 a.m.
