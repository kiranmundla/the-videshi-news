Ravindra Jadeja Scripts History With 350th Test Wicket in Galle
  • Ravindra Jadeja became the fourth cricketer in history to score 4,000+ runs and take 350+ wickets in Test cricket
  • He joins Kapil Dev (5,248 runs, 434 wickets), Ian Botham (5,200 runs, 383 wickets), and Daniel Vettori (4,531 runs, 362 wickets) in the elite club
  • Jadeja reached the milestone on Day 3 of the first Test against Sri Lanka in Galle, with 4,108 runs and 350 wickets in just 90 Tests
  • He is the only member of this quartet to achieve the double before playing 100 Test matches

Ravindra Jadeja reached a historic milestone on the third day of the first Test against Sri Lanka in Galle on Monday, becoming only the fourth cricketer in Test history to score at least 4,000 runs and take 350 or more wickets in the longest format, according to the Times of India.

The 36-year-old left-arm spinner picked up his second wicket of the innings when Sri Lanka's Keshara Nuwantha attempted to slap-drive an away-turner through the off side. The ball took a deflection off his knee and ballooned towards Shubman Gill, who completed the catch to seal the record, according to the Times of India.

An Elite Club of Four

Jadeja's achievement places him alongside three legendary all-rounders who have managed the same rare double:

Kapil Dev (India) — 5,248 runs and 434 wickets in 131 Tests
Ian Botham (England) — 5,200 runs and 383 wickets in 102 Tests
Daniel Vettori (New Zealand) — 4,531 runs and 362 wickets in 113 Tests
Ravindra Jadeja (India) — 4,108 runs and 350 wickets in 90 Tests

What sets Jadeja apart is efficiency.
