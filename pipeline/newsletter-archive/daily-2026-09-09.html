BRICS Summit 2026: Who's Coming to New Delhi and What's at Stake for India
  • India hosts the 18th BRICS Summit at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, on September 12–13, with leaders from all 11 member nations expected to attend.
  • Chinese President Xi Jinping's likely visit would be his first trip to India since 2019, with a Modi-Xi bilateral meeting widely anticipated on the sidelines.
  • Russia's Kremlin said President Putin will hold talks with Modi on trade, rare earth cooperation and defence, with 90% of BRICS transactions already conducted in national currencies.
  • The summit faces a consensus test after the May foreign ministers' meeting failed to produce a joint communiqué for the first time during an Indian chairship year, fracturing over Middle East divisions.

India is set to host the 18th BRICS Summit at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi on September 12–13, bringing together leaders from across the expanded 11-member bloc in what will be the group's most consequential gathering since its 2024 enlargement. The summit marks 20 years since Brazil, Russia, India and China first formalised the grouping, and it arrives at a moment when the bloc's ability to maintain consensus faces its most serious test.

Who's in the Room

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will chair the summit under India's fourth BRICS presidency, themed "Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability." The confirmed and expected attendee list reads like a who's who of the non-Western world: Chinese President Xi Jinping, Russian President Vladimir Putin, Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian, South African President Cyril Ramaphosa, Egyptian President Abdel Fattah el-Sisi, Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto, Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed, Belarusian President Alexander Lukashenko, Kazakh President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev, Malaysian Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim and Thai Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul.
