Schengen Visa Rejections Cost Indian Travellers Rs 154 Crore Despite Record Applications
  • More than 181,000 Schengen visa applications from India were rejected in 2025, costing applicants an estimated Rs 154 crore (€16 million) in non-refundable fees
  • Slovenia had the highest rejection rate for Indian applicants at 46.1 percent, followed by Bulgaria at 37 percent and Greece at 33 percent, while Germany maintained a relatively low 10.5 percent rate despite processing over 153,000 applications
  • Insufficient or inconsistent financial proof was the leading cause of rejections, accounting for more than 30 percent of refusals in a review of 12,000 Indian cases

Indian travellers lost an estimated Rs 154 crore in non-refundable visa fees after more than 181,000 Schengen visa applications were rejected in 2025, according to data released by the European Commission. The figure represents one of the largest financial hits from visa denials for any nationality globally.

Out of more than 11.5 lakh applications filed by Indians last year — a record — roughly one in every six was denied.
