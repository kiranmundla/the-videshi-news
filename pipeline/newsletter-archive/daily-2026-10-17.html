Why it matters
The release of *Prahaar* serves as a powerful reminder of the resilience of the Indian legal system in the face of terror, highlighting the often-overlooked narratives behind monumental events like the 26/11 attacks. For the Indian diaspora in the US, this film not only rekindles a shared memory but also sparks discussions around justice and representation, emphasizing the importance of understanding the complexities of such crucial historical moments. Engaging with this film could foster a deeper connection to one's roots and an appreciation for the legal battles that shaped contemporary India.
