Russian drones kill 2 at Ukraine-Moldova border crossing; Zelenskyy demands global action
  • Russian drones struck the Starokozache border crossing in Ukraine's Odesa region on Wednesday, killing two civilians and injuring three; the checkpoint, about a kilometre from Moldovan territory, was temporarily shut.
  • The attack is part of a pattern: Ukrainian border guards say Russia has hit five border crossings in recent weeks — four on the Moldova border and one on the Romanian border.
  • Separately, overnight Russian strikes on Kyiv killed one person and wounded 14, as Moscow resumed near-constant attacks on the capital after a brief pause during a visit by US peace negotiators.
  • A day earlier, Russian drones violated Moldovan airspace, forcing Chișinău airport to close and delaying President Volodymyr Zelenskyy's departure for Norway, where he attended King Harald V's funeral.

What: Russian drone strikes on the Ukraine-Moldova border crossing and Kyiv, killing two at the border and one in the capital. When: Overnight Tuesday into Wednesday, September 9.
