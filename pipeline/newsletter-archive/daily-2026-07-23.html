Monsoon Fury Batters Gujarat: One Dead, Over 1,200 Rescued as South Gujarat Drowns
  • At least one person killed in Tapi district as over 1,200 people rescued across rain-battered south Gujarat, with 50+ cars submerged in a Valsad parking lot.
  • Kaprada taluka in Valsad received 16 inches (397mm) of rain in 24 hours; Chikhli and Vansda in Navsari each recorded 10 inches.
  • Over 400 roads closed across Valsad and Navsari districts, railway services disrupted, and schools shut as IMD issues red alert for continued extremely heavy rainfall.
  • 33 NDRF and SDRF teams deployed across the state as multiple rivers—including the Kaveri, Auranga, and Purna—breach or approach danger levels.

Relentless monsoon rains have unleashed devastation across south Gujarat, killing at least one person in Tapi district and forcing the rescue of more than 1,200 others as rivers swelled past danger marks and entire neighborhoods went underwater, according to NDTV and The Hindu.

The worst-hit areas span Valsad, Navsari, Surat, and Tapi districts, where a 24-hour deluge ending on July 22 dumped rainfall measured in feet rather than inches. Kaprada taluka in Valsad recorded the highest single-day total at a staggering 16 inches (397mm), while Nanapondha registered 11 inches, according to the State Emergency Operations Centre (SEOC).

A Parking Lot Turned Lake

Viral footage from Valsad showed more than 50 cars submerged in a flooded parking lot, a scene NDTV described as emblematic of the monsoon's fury.
