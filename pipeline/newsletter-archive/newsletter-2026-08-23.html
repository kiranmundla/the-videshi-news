"Talent Is Universal, Opportunity Is Not": Wadhwani Foundation CEO Dr. Ajay Kela on Why the Diaspora's Greatest Asset Isn't Money
  • The diaspora's greatest assets are knowledge, networks, and experience — not financial capital
  • India's "missing middle" — growth-stage companies in tier-2 and tier-3 cities — represents a massive untapped opportunity for impact
  • India doesn't have a research problem; it has a translation problem — turning lab discoveries into products and companies
  • The next generation of globally significant deep-tech companies will increasingly be built in India, for the world

Every year, thousands of accomplished Indians abroad talk about "giving back" to India. Most never get past the talking.
