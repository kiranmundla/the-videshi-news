🛂 IMMIGRATION — TOP STORY
DHS Ends Duration of Status for F-1 and J-1 Visas, Imposes Four-Year Cap
  • The DHS final rule, published July 17, eliminates “duration of status” for F-1, J-1, and I visa holders and caps initial stays at four years, taking effect September 15, 2026.
  • Students needing additional time must apply to USCIS for extensions, with requests subject to background checks, biometric screening, and fraud reviews — a shift from the current university-managed system.
  • The post-graduation grace period for F-1 students is cut from 60 days to 30 days, and restrictions on changing majors, transferring schools, and pursuing additional degrees are tightened.
  • India, which sends the largest number of international students to the U.S. — roughly 330,000 in 2023-24 — faces an outsized impact, with New Delhi saying it is “actively monitoring” the situation.
