🍛 FOOD — TOP STORY
Bengaluru Restaurants Set August 15 Deadline to Quit Swiggy and Zomato
  • Bengaluru restaurant owners have set August 15 as a deadline to delist from Swiggy and Zomato unless commissions — which have risen from 16% to 25–30% over six years — are reduced.
  • The revolt follows Namakkal, Tamil Nadu, where nearly every restaurant pulled off the platforms on July 1, switching to local delivery service Zaaroz.
  • Rapido's zero-commission platform Ownly, built with the National Restaurant Association of India, has onboarded roughly 20,000 restaurants in Bengaluru and is now integrated into Rapido's main app.
  • Separately, the Karnataka High Court has ordered Zomato, Swiggy, and Zepto to deposit gig worker welfare fees, with a hearing set for August 14.

Bengaluru restaurant owners have drawn a line: lower the commissions by August 15, India's Independence Day, or they walk. The deadline, set by local restaurant associations, marks the latest escalation in a nationwide standoff between India's food industry and the delivery platforms that dominate it.

The symbolism of the date is hard to miss.
