WHITE HOUSE CLEARS PERM GREEN CARD OVERHAUL

The Department of Labor's proposed PERM overhaul cleared White House review on October 23 and heads to the Federal Register, covering recruitment standards, layoff rules and nondiscrimination requirements.

Current PERM filings continue under the existing rules until a final rule is issued.
