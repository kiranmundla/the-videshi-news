CHEVRON EARNINGS BEAT ESTIMATES AS REFINING LIFTS PROFIT TO $9.9 BILLION ON $67.8 BILLION REVENUE

Chevron reported third-quarter earnings of $9.9 billion, up from $3.5 billion a year earlier, as revenue rose 36.3% to $67.8 billion on a refining surge.

Chevron declared a $1.78 quarterly dividend payable December 10.
