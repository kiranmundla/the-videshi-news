INDIA ON TRACK TO HIT $1 TRILLION IN EXPORTS THIS YEAR

Commerce Secretary Rajesh Agrawal said India is on track to hit $1 trillion in exports of goods and services this year, after October exports hit a record $89.99 billion.

The $1 trillion figure is a target for the financial year ending March 31, 2027, not yet a reported outcome.
