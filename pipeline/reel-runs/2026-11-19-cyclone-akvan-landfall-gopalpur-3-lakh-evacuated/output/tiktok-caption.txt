CYCLONE AKVAN MAKES LANDFALL NEAR GOPALPUR IN ODISHA

Cyclone Akvan began landfall near Gopalpur on the Odisha coast on Friday morning, crossing at 70-80 kmph and gusting to 90 kmph after weakening from a severe cyclonic storm, according to the India Meteorological Department.

Odisha's government is running a zero-casualty mission, and no casualties had been reported as landfall began.
