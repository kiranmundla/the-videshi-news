CANADA'S IMMIGRATION BACKLOG FALLS TO 1.51 MILLION

Canada's immigration department, IRCC, finalized 3,001,860 applications in the first nine months of 2026 and cut the number of files awaiting a final decision to 1,508,675 by September 30.

Family sponsorship was the only major stream that grew, rising by 1,685 to 168,855 files.
