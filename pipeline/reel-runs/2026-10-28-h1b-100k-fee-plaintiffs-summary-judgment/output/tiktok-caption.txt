PLAINTIFFS WANT A FAST RULING ON THE $100,000 H-1B FEE

Employers and workers in Global Nurse Force v. Trump have asked a California federal court to move quickly toward summary judgment on the $100,000 H-1B fee, while the Trump administration wants the case paused until the Ninth Circuit decides its appeal.

Petitions are being processed without the fee for now, but the relief rests on preliminary orders an appeals court could undo.
