ABBVIE EARNINGS BEAT ESTIMATES, REVENUE RISES 10% TO $17.4 BILLION AND 2026 EPS GUIDANCE IS RAISED

AbbVie reported third-quarter adjusted earnings of $3.82 per share, beating the $3.74 consensus, on revenue of $17.404 billion, up 10.3%, powered by Skyrizi and Rinvoq.

AbbVie raised full-year 2026 adjusted EPS guidance to $13.95-$13.99 and lifted its revenue outlook to about $67.8 billion.
