INDIA APPROVES ITS FIRST DENGUE VACCINE

India's drug regulator, the DCGI, has granted market authorisation to Takeda's Qdenga — the first dengue vaccine approved in India — for people aged 4 to 60.

Takeda expects to launch Qdenga in India in the first half of 2027; pricing has not been finalised.
