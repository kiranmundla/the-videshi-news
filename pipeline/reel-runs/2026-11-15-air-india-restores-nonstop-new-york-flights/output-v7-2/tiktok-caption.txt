AIR INDIA RESTORES NON-STOP FLIGHTS TO NEW YORK

Air India has restored non-stop flights on all four India-New York routes - Delhi and Mumbai to JFK and Newark - ending the Rome refuelling stop, the airline said on November 15.

Journey times fall by up to five hours with the Rome stop discontinued.
