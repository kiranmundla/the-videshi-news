13 LAWMAKERS SEEK FEDERAL PROBE INTO SEATTLE DIWALI EVENT DISRUPTION

Thirteen US lawmakers, including all six Indian American House members, have asked the Justice Department to investigate whether the November 7 disruption of a Diwali festival in Seattle violated federal civil rights laws.

The Justice Department has not publicly responded, and the lawmakers have asked for a briefing by December 4.
