H-1B WORKERS FACE A 60-DAY CLOCK AT MICROSOFT

Microsoft has offered a Voluntary Exit Offer to about 60,000 employees, but has not said whether H-1B holders are eligible. For visa workers who accept and leave on December 31, USCIS rules give up to 60 days to find a new sponsor, change status, or depart.

Microsoft has not answered the visa question — and the clock starts when employment ends, not at the December decision deadline.
