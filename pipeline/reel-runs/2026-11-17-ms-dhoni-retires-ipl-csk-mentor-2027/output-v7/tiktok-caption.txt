DHONI ENDS 19-YEAR IPL CAREER, STAYS AS CSK MENTOR

Chennai Super Kings announced on Tuesday, November 17, that MS Dhoni has retired from the IPL as a player and will begin a new chapter with the franchise as Team Mentor from IPL 2027.

Dhoni will still be in the CSK dugout when IPL 2027 begins — in the mentor's seat, not at the toss.
