GOLDMAN SACHS UPGRADES INDIA TO OVERWEIGHT, SETS NIFTY TARGET OF 28,500

Goldman Sachs upgraded Indian equities to overweight in its Asia-Pacific portfolio strategy and set a Nifty target of 28,500 by end-2027, implying roughly 12 percent upside from current levels.

The 28,500 target is a forecast, not a result, and rests on 15% earnings growth in 2027 and 2028.
