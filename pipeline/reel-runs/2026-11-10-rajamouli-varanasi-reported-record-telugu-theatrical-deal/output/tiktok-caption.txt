RAJAMOULI'S VARANASI REPORTEDLY SETS RECORD TELUGU THEATRICAL DEAL

SS Rajamouli's Telugu film Varanasi, starring Mahesh Babu, has reportedly closed its Telugu-states theatrical deal at Rs. 300 crore according to 123Telugu, with other trade outlets reporting Rs. 290 crore and official confirmation still awaited.

On either reported figure, the deal would exceed the roughly Rs. 225 crore reported for RRR.
