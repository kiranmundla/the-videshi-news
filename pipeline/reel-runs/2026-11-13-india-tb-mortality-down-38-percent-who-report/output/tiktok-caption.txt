INDIA TB MORTALITY FALLS 38 PERCENT

India recorded a 38% decline in tuberculosis mortality since 2015, compared with a global decline of 35%, according to the WHO Global Tuberculosis Report 2026.

India met 96% of TB programme funding requirements through domestic resources.
