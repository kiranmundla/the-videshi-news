JAILER 2 HINDI EARNS RS 43.8 CRORE IN 15 DAYS, BOOKS 68% PROFIT OVER BREAKEVEN

The Hindi dubbed version of Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has earned Rs 43.8 crore net in India in 15 days, booking a profit of Rs 17.8 crore, or 68.46 percent, over its Rs 26 crore breakeven, according to Koimoi.

The Hindi version needs Rs 52 crore net for a hit verdict, with about six days of open run before Ramayana Part One releases.
