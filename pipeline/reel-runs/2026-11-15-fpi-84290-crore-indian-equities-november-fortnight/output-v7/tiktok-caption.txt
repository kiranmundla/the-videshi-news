FOREIGN INVESTORS PUMP ₹84,290 CRORE INTO INDIAN STOCKS

Foreign portfolio investors put a net ₹84,290 crore into Indian equities between November 2 and 13, reversing two straight months of outflows, depository data reported by PTI shows.

FPIs remain net sellers for 2026 overall, despite the inflow.
