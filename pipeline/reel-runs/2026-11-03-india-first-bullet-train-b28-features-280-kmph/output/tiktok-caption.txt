INDIA'S FIRST BULLET TRAIN REVEALED

India's first indigenous bullet train, the B28, is being built by BEML in Bengaluru for the Mumbai-Ahmedabad corridor, with a maximum test speed of 280 kmph and a planned operational speed of 249 kmph.

The B28 marks a step toward domestic manufacturing capability in high-speed rail.
