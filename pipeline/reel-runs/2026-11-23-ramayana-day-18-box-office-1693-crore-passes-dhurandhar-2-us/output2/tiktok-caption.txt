RAMAYANA PASSES DHURANDHAR 2 IN THE US, NEARS RS 1,700 CRORE WORLDWIDE

Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana has reached Rs 1,693.50 crore worldwide gross in 18 days, according to trade tracker Sacnilk, and its $28.4 million US total has passed Dhurandhar: The Revenge's $28.1 million to become the highest-grossing Indian film ever in the US.

For US audiences, the argument is already settled: the biggest Indian film in American box office history is a Hindi-language mythological epic, three weekends into its run.
