FOREIGN INVESTORS PUMP $7.5 BILLION INTO INDIAN EQUITIES IN TEN SESSIONS

Foreign portfolio investors pumped $7.5 billion into Indian equities in the first 10 trading sessions of November, the highest monthly inflow in five years, reversing a year of heavy FPI selling.

With more than a week of November trading left, the inflow is chasing November 2020's $9.6 billion monthly record.
