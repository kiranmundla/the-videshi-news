TATA STEEL Q2 PROFIT RISES 16.2%

Tata Steel's consolidated profit after tax for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 rose 16.2% year on year to Rs 3,699.59 crore, on revenue up 11.5%.

India carried the quarter, with India EBITDA of Rs 10,634 crore at a margin of 25%.
