EARNINGS FALL, BUYBACKS HIT $9.6 BILLION

Berkshire Hathaway's third-quarter operating earnings fell 5.8% to $12.705 billion as insurance underwriting profit fell 43%, while the company bought back about $9.6 billion of its own stock.

Buybacks may not keep this pace — Berkshire is price-sensitive, and October buying already slowed as the share price rose.
