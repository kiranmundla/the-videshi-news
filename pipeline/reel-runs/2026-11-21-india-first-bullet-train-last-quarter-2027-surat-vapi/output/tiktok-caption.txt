INDIA'S FIRST BULLET TRAIN RUNS IN LATE 2027

India's first high-speed rail service will commence in the last quarter of 2027, with the Surat-Vapi section of the Mumbai-Ahmedabad corridor opening first.

For travellers, nothing can be booked yet: the first service is a Gujarat section, not a Mumbai-Ahmedabad through journey.
