RUPEE HITS SEVEN-MONTH HIGH NEAR ₹93.40, SQUEEZING IT EXPORTERS' EARNINGS

The Indian rupee climbed to around ₹93.39–₹93.41 per US dollar on November 18, its strongest level in seven months, squeezing the rupee earnings of TCS, Infosys and other IT exporters.

The rupee remains about 4% weaker than on January 1, when a dollar cost ₹89.98.
