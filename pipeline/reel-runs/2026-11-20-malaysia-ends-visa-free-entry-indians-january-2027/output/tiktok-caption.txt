MALAYSIA ENDS VISA-FREE ENTRY FOR INDIANS FROM JANUARY 1, 2027

Malaysia will end visa-free entry for Indian passport holders from January 1, 2027; travellers arriving on or after that date must obtain a visa in advance through the MyVISA portal, with applications opening December 1, 2026.

Until December 31, 2026, Indians can still enter visa-free for up to 30 days — from January 1, 2027, a visa obtained before travel is required.
