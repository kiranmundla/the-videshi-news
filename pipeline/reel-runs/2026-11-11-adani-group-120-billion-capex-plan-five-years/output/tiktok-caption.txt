ADANI GROUP UNVEILS $120 BILLION CAPEX PLAN OVER FIVE YEARS

Adani Group executives told Fortune India they plan to invest about $120 billion in capital expenditure over the next five years, more than $20 billion a year, across all group businesses.

The figures are a plan, not a result: the group estimates the spending would double its asset base to Rs 18 lakh crore.
