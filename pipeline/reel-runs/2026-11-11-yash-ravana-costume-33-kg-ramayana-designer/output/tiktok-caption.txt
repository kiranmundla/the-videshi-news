YASH WORE RAVANA COSTUMES WEIGHING UP TO 33 KG IN RAMAYANA

Costume designer Saniya Sardhariya told India Today that Yash's costumes as Ravana in Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana typically weighed 16 to 20 kg including jewellery, with the heaviest outfits close to 30 to 33 kg.

Sardhariya said Yash kept a cheerful atmosphere on set despite the weight.
