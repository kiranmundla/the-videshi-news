RAMAYANA OPENS WITH BOLLYWOOD'S SECOND-BEST ADVANCE EVER

Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana, starring Ranbir Kapoor, Yash and Sai Pallavi, closed its day 1 advance booking at Rs 42.96 crore gross in India excluding blocked seats, from 12.14 lakh tickets.

It is the highest pre-sales ever for a non-sequel.
