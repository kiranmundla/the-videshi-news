RUPEE SETTLES 65 PAISE HIGHER AT 92.67 TO THE DOLLAR

The rupee settled 65 paise higher at 92.67 (provisional) against the US dollar on Friday, November 20, up from a close of 94.19 on November 13, powered by foreign inflows and a rally in domestic stocks.

Whether the rupee holds will depend on the foreign inflows continuing — those flows reversed to net withdrawals in September and October.
