A. R. RAHMAN SPENT THREE YEARS ON 'RAMAYANA' MUSIC, TURNED DOWN 10–12 FILMS

A. R. Rahman told a press conference at the International Film Festival of India (IFFI) in Panaji that he has spent nearly three years on the music of Nitesh Tiwari's two-part 'Ramayana', turned down 10–12 film projects to stay focused on it, and is composing the score with Hans Zimmer.

Three years of music work went into 'Ramayana', most of it before a frame reached the screen.
