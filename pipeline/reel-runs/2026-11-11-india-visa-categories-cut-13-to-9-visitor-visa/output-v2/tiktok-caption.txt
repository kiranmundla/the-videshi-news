INDIA CUTS VISA CATEGORIES FROM 13 TO NINE, RENAMES TOURIST VISA AS VISITOR VISA

India will consolidate its 13 main visa categories into nine and cut sub-categories from 49 to 23 under a Union Home Ministry review of the Visa Manual, 2019, renaming the Tourist Visa as the Visitor Visa.

Applicants should keep using the current categories until the new structure is notified.
