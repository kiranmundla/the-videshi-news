IMMIGRATION COURT DEADLINES CUT TO 10 DAYS

A new EOIR policy memo, effective November 23, cuts filing deadlines in US immigration court removal cases to 10 days for represented respondents.

Miss the deadline and the application is deemed waived; DHS can seek a final order of removability.
