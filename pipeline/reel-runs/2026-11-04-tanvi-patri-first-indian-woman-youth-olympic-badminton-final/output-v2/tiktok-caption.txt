TANVI PATRI, 15, FIRST INDIAN WOMAN IN YOUTH OLYMPIC BADMINTON FINAL

Fifteen-year-old Tanvi Patri beat Japan's second seed Yuzuno Watanabe 21-14, 11-21, 21-19 in a 54-minute semifinal to become the first Indian woman to reach a Youth Olympic Games badminton final.

The win guarantees India its first-ever women's singles badminton medal at the Youth Olympic Games.
