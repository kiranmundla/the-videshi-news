GALAXEYE AWARDS BELLATRIX PROPULSION CONTRACT FOR ITS NEXT 30 SATELLITES

Bengaluru-based GalaxEye has awarded Bellatrix Aerospace a multi-year contract to supply Arka electric propulsion systems for its next 30 Drishti Gen-II satellites, in a deal the two startups describe as India's largest private space order by satellite count.

The test now is delivery: the first two propulsion systems ship first, and the 30-satellite constellation is a 2030 target, not a fleet in orbit.
