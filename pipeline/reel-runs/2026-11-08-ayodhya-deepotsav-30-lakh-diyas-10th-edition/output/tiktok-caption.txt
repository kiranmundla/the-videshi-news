AYODHYA LIGHTS OVER 30 LAKH DIYAS

More than 30 lakh earthen lamps were lit across 56 ghats along the Saryu in Ayodhya on Saturday, November 7, for the 10th Deepotsav.

The official Guinness tally is still awaited against the 2025 record of 26.17 lakh lamps.
