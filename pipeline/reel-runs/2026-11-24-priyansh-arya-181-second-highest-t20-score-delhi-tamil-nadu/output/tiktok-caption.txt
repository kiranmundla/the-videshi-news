181 OFF 68 BALLS: SECOND-HIGHEST SCORE IN T20 HISTORY

Delhi captain Priyansh Arya scored 181 off 68 balls against Tamil Nadu in the Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy in Indore, the second-highest score in men's T20 history, as Delhi won by 116 runs.

Arya finished seven runs short of Lhuan-dre Pretorius's world record of 188 not out.
