FIRST SOUTH ASIAN AMERICAN ELECTED TO CONGRESS FROM ARIZONA

Dr Amish Shah, a Democrat, won Arizona's 1st Congressional District with 52.4% of the vote, becoming the first South Asian American to represent Arizona in the US Congress.

Amish Shah takes office on January 3, when the new Congress convenes.
