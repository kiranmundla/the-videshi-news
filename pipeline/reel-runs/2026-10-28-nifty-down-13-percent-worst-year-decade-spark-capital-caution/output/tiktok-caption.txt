NIFTY FALLS 13% IN 2026

India's Nifty 50 has fallen about 13% this year and is on course for its worst annual performance in over a decade, with Spark Capital telling Reuters the slide may not yet be a buying opportunity.

A technical rebound is plausible in November, but a durable re-rating needs earnings to hold, crude to ease and foreign money to return.
