ALBERTA INVITES 425 WORKERS IN FIVE DRAWS

Alberta issued 425 invitations to apply for provincial nomination across five Alberta Advantage Immigration Program draws between October 23 and November 2, led by the Rural Renewal Stream with 210 invitations.

Only 495 of Alberta's 6,603 nomination spaces for 2026 remain, with 32,211 candidates still active in the pool.
