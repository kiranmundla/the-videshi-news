INDIA CROSSES 1 CRORE HPV VACCINE DOSES IN UNDER NINE MONTHS

India has administered more than 1 crore HPV vaccine doses in less than nine months under the National HPV Vaccination Campaign, reaching nearly 87% of the annual target cohort of about 1.2 crore 14-year-old girls.

The campaign offers a free, single-dose vaccine to 14-year-old girls at government health facilities, with parental consent.
