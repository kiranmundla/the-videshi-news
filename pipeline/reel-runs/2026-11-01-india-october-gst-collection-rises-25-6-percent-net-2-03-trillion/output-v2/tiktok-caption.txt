INDIA'S NET GST COLLECTION JUMPS 25.6% TO RS 2.03 TRILLION IN OCTOBER

India's net GST collection rose 25.6% year on year to Rs 2.03 trillion in October, according to a government statement reported by Reuters, with gross collection up 22.9% to Rs 2.31 trillion.

October was the fourth month of this financial year in which gross GST crossed Rs 2 trillion — a sign of sustained consumption and compliance, not a one-off spike.
