ANIL MENON COMPLETES NASA'S 100TH US SPACEWALK

NASA astronaut Anil Menon and Canada's Joshua Kutryk spent 7 hours and 11 minutes outside the International Space Station on October 29, installing its seventh roll-out solar array.

Once the eighth and final array is installed on November 5, the upgrade will raise the station's available power by up to 30 percent.
