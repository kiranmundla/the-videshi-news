New York Bans Horse-Drawn Carriages Under Romanch's Law

The New York City Council voted 40 to 7 to phase out horse-drawn carriages by June 1, 2028, naming the law for Romanch Mahajan, 18, of Pathankot, who died in June when a carriage horse bolted in Central Park.

For the Mahajan family, one outcome is already fixed: the name on New York's law is their son's.
