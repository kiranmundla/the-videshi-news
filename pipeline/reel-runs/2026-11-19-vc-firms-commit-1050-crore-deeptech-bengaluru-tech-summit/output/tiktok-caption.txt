24 VC FIRMS COMMIT ₹1,050 CRORE TO DEEPTECH STARTUPS AT BENGALURU TECH SUMMIT

Twenty-four venture capital firms announced investment commitments totalling ₹1,050.42 crore in deeptech startups at the Future Makers Conclave of the Bengaluru Tech Summit on Thursday.

The commitments are announcements, not disbursements: no allocation or payout schedule was shared at the event.
