JAILER 2 FIRST INDIAN FILM TO CROSS RS 75 CRORE IN ALL FOUR SOUTH MARKETS

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has become the first Indian film to gross over Rs 75 crore individually in each of the four major South Indian markets, according to Sacnilk.

In Kerala, it ended Baahubali 2's nine-year run as the top non-Malayalam film.
