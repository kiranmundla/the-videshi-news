## State Legislators (Highlights)

### State Senators (15+)

Key figures include **Nikil Saval** (PA), **Niraj Antani** (OH, Republican), **Kevin Thomas** (NY — first Indian-American in NY State Senate), **Vin Gopal** (NJ), **Jay Chaudhuri** (NC), **Ram Villivalam** (IL — first in IL), **Sam Singh** (MI), **Manka Dhingra** (WA — her 2017 win flipped the WA Senate), **Kesha Ram Hinsdale** (VT), and others across CT, VA, and NJ.

### State Representatives / Assembly Members (25+)

Notable members include **Kumar Barve** (MD — first Indian-American state legislator in US history, since 1990), **Nabeela Syed** (IL — youngest member of the IL General Assembly at 23), **Jenifer Rajkumar** (NY — first South Asian American woman in NY State Legislature, led Diwali holiday effort), **Jasmeet Bains** (CA — first Sikh woman in CA Legislature), **Arvind Venkat** (PA — first Indian-American in PA House), **Vandana Slatter** (WA), **Nima Kulkarni** (KY), **Padma Kuppa** (MI — first Hindu in MI legislature), and many others across TX, OH, NV, NJ, CT, IA, and TN.

**Bipartisan representation:** While the majority are Democrats, notable Republicans include **Niraj Antani** (OH State Senate), **Harry Arora** (CT), **Anil Beephan Jr.** (NY), and **Sabi Kumar** (TN).

## Key Trends

1. **Record representation in Congress:** Six Indian Americans in the U.S. House is an all-time high, all Democrats.
2. **Statewide breakthroughs:** Ghazala Hashmi's 2025 Virginia Lt. Gov. win was the first Indian-American statewide win in the state, and the first Muslim woman to win statewide anywhere in the US.
3. **Executive branch visibility:** Usha Vance as Second Lady and Kash Patel as FBI Director represent the highest-profile Indian-American appointments in the current administration.
4. **Generational shift:** Young leaders like Nabeela Syed (23 when elected), Zohran Mamdani (NYC Mayor), and Suhas Subramanyam are bringing new energy.
5. **Geographic diversification:** While historically concentrated in CA, NJ, and IL, Indian-American representation is expanding to states like VA, OH, NC, ME, KY, IA, and TN.
6. **Bipartisan presence:** While Democrats dominate, Republicans like Nikki Haley, Bobby Jindal, Vivek Ramaswamy, Niraj Antani, Sabi Kumar, and Kash Patel show the community's political diversity.
7. **Mayoral milestones:** Zohran Mamdani leading America's largest city is a landmark moment; Nithya Raman's LA mayor runoff could add another major city.
