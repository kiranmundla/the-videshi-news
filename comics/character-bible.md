# The Videshi Comics — Character Bible

**Brand**: "Friday Laughs" by The Videshi Comics — Life Abroad. Satire Included.
**Format**: 4-panel comic strip, numbered panels, yellow punchline banner at bottom
**Style**: Clean cartoon/manga-influenced, warm colors, Bay Area setting details (Transamerica Pyramid, SF skyline)

---

## Core Cast

### Raj Mehta — "The Optimist"
- Late 20s Indian-American tech worker in the Bay Area
- Look: Messy black hair, rectangular glasses, navy blue hoodie, slim build
- Always has a MacBook, coffee mug with funny slogans ("FOCUS DELIVER REPEAT", "INR STRONG HERE")
- Personality: Earnest, slightly naïve, still adjusting to American prices and systems after years here
- His H-1B/green card journey is a running theme — always checking USCIS, always waiting
- Comic role: The guy things happen TO. Reacts with wide eyes and dramatic expressions.

### Priya Mehta — "The Realist"
- Late 20s, Raj's wife
- Look: Long dark wavy hair, green top/casual Indian-modern style, expressive face
- Personality: Sharp, practical, the one who says what everyone's thinking. Handles immigration paperwork, knows every desi grocery deal in a 20-mile radius
- Comic role: The voice of reason that makes the punchline land. Deadpan delivery.

### Amma (Raj's Mom) — "The WhatsApp Oracle"
- Mid 50s, back in India
- Look: Traditional Indian salwar/sari, warm face, always shown on a phone screen or WhatsApp call
- Personality: Compares everything to India prices, asks when they're coming back, sends 47 Good Morning messages before 7 AM, forwards unverified health tips
- Signature lines: "Beta, when are you coming?", "In India, tomatoes are just ₹40!"
- Comic role: The voice from home that puts everything in perspective (whether you want it or not)

### Vikram ("Vik") — "The Veteran"
- Mid 30s, Raj's coworker/friend, been in the US for 12 years
- Look: Slightly stocky, beard, polo shirt or button-down, always confident posture
- Personality: Has opinions about everything. Got his green card "the hard way" and won't let you forget it. Knows every immigration lawyer, every loophole, every desi restaurant worth driving 45 minutes for.
- Comic role: The know-it-all who's sometimes right and sometimes hilariously wrong

### Dadi (Raj's Grandmother) — "The Philosopher"
- 70s, visits from India occasionally
- Look: White hair in a bun, reading glasses, simple cotton sari
- Personality: Drops unexpected wisdom. Confused by American things but somehow makes more sense than everyone else.
- Comic role: Surprise punchline delivery. Appears less often but steals every strip she's in.

---

## Recurring Elements

- **Mug slogans**: Every strip, Raj's coffee mug has a different funny slogan
- **WhatsApp UI**: Mom's messages shown as realistic WhatsApp bubbles
- **Bay Area landmarks**: SF skyline, Transamerica Pyramid visible through apartment window
- **Indian touches**: Small plant on desk, framed family photo, Indian décor mixed with IKEA
- **Yellow punchline banner**: Every strip ends with a one-liner caption in a yellow bar at the bottom
- **Branding**: "the Videshi comics" logo top-right, "FRIDAY LAUGHS" header top-left

---

## Topic Categories (for weekly rotation)

1. Immigration & visa struggles (green card wait, H-1B lottery, USCIS delays)
2. Grocery/cost of living shock (US vs India prices)
3. Family calls from India (WhatsApp, time zones, mom's advice)
4. Tech life in Silicon Valley (standups, layoffs, side projects)
5. Cultural code-switching (office vs home, American holidays vs Indian ones)
6. Food & cooking abroad (finding ingredients, explaining Indian food to Americans)
7. Housing & rent in the Bay Area
8. Driving & commuting (Bay Bridge traffic, Caltrain)
9. Parenting as immigrants (school system, playdates, explaining festivals)
10. Return-to-India debates ("should we go back?")
